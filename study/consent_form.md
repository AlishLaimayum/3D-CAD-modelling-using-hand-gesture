### Purpose of Research
You are invited to participate in a research study evaluating an experimental contactless 3D CAD modeling system operated via webcam hand tracking compared to standard mouse/keyboard input.

### Description of Procedures
If you agree to participate, you will:
1. Complete a brief demographics and experience survey (approx. 5 minutes).
2. Receive training on both the gesture-controlled and mouse-based CAD systems (approx. 10 minutes).
3. Perform four standardized 3D geometric modeling tasks using both input techniques (approx. 20–25 minutes).
4. Complete post-task questionnaires regarding usability, cognitive workload, and arm fatigue (approx. 10 minutes).
Total session duration: Approximately 45 to 60 minutes.

### Voluntary Participation & Right to Withdraw
Your participation in this study is entirely voluntary. You may decline to answer any question, take breaks at any time, or withdraw from the study at any point without penalty or loss of benefits.

### Risks and Discomforts
The gesture input involves mid-air arm movements. You may experience mild arm or shoulder fatigue (commonly referred to as "gorilla arm" fatigue). Mandatory resting intervals are scheduled between tasks to mitigate fatigue. You may request additional rest breaks at any point.

### Confidentiality and Data Protection
All data collected during this study will be strictly anonymized. You will be assigned a random participant identifier (e.g., `P01`, `P02`). No video or facial recordings of you will be stored; only numeric hand landmark coordinates and interaction event timestamps will be logged.
